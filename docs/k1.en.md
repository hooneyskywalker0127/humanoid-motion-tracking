# Third robot — ROBOTIS AI Sapiens K1

[← README](../README.en.md) · [한국어](k1.md)

To check that the pipeline is not just a pile of hand fixes for the second robot, a third humanoid was added: the
[ROBOTIS AI Sapiens K1](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/ai_sapiens) (23 DoF, leg length 0.80 m, about the G1's 0.79 m).
It is not on GMR's official list, so it went the same way as IGRIS-C, and that way is now the procedure in
[docs/new-robot.en.md](new-robot.en.md).

## Registration

- **GMR**: `robotis_k1`. The MJCF is `k1.xml` from the public repository; the IK table started as the G1 table with only the wrist link
  names changed (dimensions within 1 cm of the G1). The config in use is `configs/gmr/bvh_lafan1_to_k1_candidate.json`, and
  `scripts/newrobot/make_ik_config.py` produces the same table from the MJCF.
- **Isaac Lab** (fork `whole_body_tracking`): `robots/k1.py` (URDF `package://` paths made absolute by `scripts/k1/prepare_urdf.py`),
  `tasks/tracking/config/k1/` (`Tracking-Flat-K1-v0`), `csv_to_npz.py --robot k1`. PD gains follow the G1 ratio
  (kp = G1 kp/torque ratio × K1 actuator torque, kd = 0.0637·kp; QC080 96.9 Nm, QC060 47.3 Nm). Self-collision off.

## Retargeting v1

The same recipe as IGRIS-C [v4](igris.en.md#retargeting-v4--human-sized-jumps-dance2-0--100): GMR as in the paper (pelvis position
weight 100, per-frame grounding, ground_height 0.03) + contact-gated foot weights 100/20 (human foot within 8 cm) + trunk (pelvis,
torso) rotation weight 100 + arm joint velocity limit 10 rad/s + continuity term 1.0. The candidate before it, P10 (stage-2 pelvis
weight 20, grounding smoothed over 0.5 s), inflated the dance2 jumps (pelvis apex +0.41 m, human +0.33) and scored 0%.
Two differences from IGRIS-C: a trunk weight of 200 made both arms and legs worse on the K1, so it stays at 100, and the leg scale is
capped at 0.90 because the jump index is below the G1's (0.35 vs 0.52).
